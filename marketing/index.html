Guides

Frequently asked questions

Will Google penalise AI-generated articles?

Google rewards helpful, original content regardless of how it was produced. Writer-GPT grounds every article in live SERP research and cited sources, and gives you an editor to review before anything is published.

How long does one article take?

A 2,000-word article takes about 60–90 seconds. Bulk runs generate in parallel, so 100 articles typically finish in under 20 minutes.

Can I use my own brand voice?

Yes. Upload a few existing posts or a style guide and Writer-GPT builds a reusable voice profile you can apply to any run.

Which CMS platforms do you support?

WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Ghost and Notion out of the box, plus a REST API and webhooks for anything else.

What happens to unused articles?

Monthly allowances reset on your billing date. Yearly plans roll unused credits forward for up to three months.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes — cancel from the billing page in one click. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for, and every article you generated stays yours.
